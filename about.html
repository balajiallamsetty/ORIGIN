Integrity

Publication Ethics & Malpractice Statement

ORIGIN strictly follows the guidelines established by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE). We enforce a zero-tolerance policy toward plagiarism, data fabrication, and redundant publications. Every paper undergoes strict automated verification against comprehensive digital archives prior to review.

ORIGIN aligns its editorial practice with widely recognized COPE guidance. This page does not claim official COPE membership.
