Before you submit

Submission Guidelines

Original work only

Authors are invited to submit their original work that has not been published or is not currently under consideration elsewhere. Manuscripts should present a clear research question, transparent methods, and findings that can be examined by independent reviewers.

A dedicated manuscript submission system is not yet available. Authors may contact the editorial office to inquire about current submission arrangements.

Contact Editorial Office

Preparation

Manuscript Structural Checklist

Use this sequence as a baseline. Journal-specific instructions will be published when individual titles launch.

01

Title Page

Concise title, full author names, institutional affiliations, and an active corresponding author email.

02

Abstract

A comprehensive, single-paragraph summary (150–250 words) detailing the background, methodology, core findings, and primary conclusions.

03

Keywords

4 to 6 specific indexing terms.

04

Main Body Text

Clearly divided into Introduction, Materials & Methods, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion.

05

References

All citations must exactly match the format of the chosen journal, such as APA, IEEE, or Vancouver. The exact reference style depends on the target journal and will be confirmed on that journal’s page.

Editorial workflow

Peer Review Process

ORIGIN uses double-blind peer review. The sequence below describes the intended editorial path from submission to publication.

  1. 1 Submission Manuscript received
  2. 2 Initial Editorial Screening Scope & integrity checks
  3. 3 Double-Blind Peer Review Independent assessment
  4. 4 Revisions & Acceptance Author response
  5. 5 Final Proof Approval Author confirmation
  6. 6 Publication Open-access release
