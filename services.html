Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ORIGIN publish its own journals?

No. ORIGIN is a research and innovation assistance organization. We help researchers prepare manuscripts and navigate the journal publication process.

Can you help me develop a research idea into a paper?

Yes. We can assist with research planning, methodology, scientific writing, editing and manuscript preparation, based on genuine research and available data.

Do you provide patent filing assistance in India?

Yes. We provide patent documentation and filing guidance, with prosecution or legal representation handled through appropriately qualified registered patent professionals where required.

Can you help with ICMR and DST funding proposals?

Yes. We assist with proposal development, research objectives, methodology, budget preparation and documentation.

Can you guarantee journal publication or patent approval?

No. Publication decisions are made by journals and patent decisions by the relevant patent authorities. We provide professional assistance and guidance but cannot guarantee acceptance or grant.

How can I submit my manuscript or proposal for initial assessment?

You can share your inquiry using our online contact form or email your abstract, manuscript draft, or research concept directly to our editorial and research support desk at Originpublicationsindia@gmail.com. Our academic team will review your requirements and provide structured guidance.
